# Limitations and responsible-AI controls

## Known limitations

- Official IFRS 9 guidance and professional interpretation are not indexed.
- Allied_REIT's exact reporting framework remains pending.
- Logistic Properties is partial and does not establish development-partner financing.
- Bounded SEC discovery can miss issuers and filing formats.
- Phrase-pattern framework checks may leave uncommon wording pending.
- Gate 5.1 post-live controller hardening was tested offline only.
- Public mode cannot reproduce private annual-report evidence text.
- Historical labels and the generation pilot retain documented provenance limits.
- Provider API cost excludes infrastructure, governance, maintenance, and review.

## Controls

- Raw annual-report and filing text stays local and is checked against outbound payloads.
- Secrets are read from environment variables and excluded from saved traces.
- Tools are allowlisted, read-only, state-bound, capped, and duplicate-protected.
- Comparison uses business, instrument, counterparty, collateral, construction, ECL, and framework dimensions.
- Reporting framework uses issuer-specific evidence or remains pending.
- Company disclosure cannot establish general IFRS requirements.
- Trace exports omit source bodies and hidden chain-of-thought.
- Private/runtime/secret paths are gitignored, and historical artifacts have a hash ledger.
- Every handoff requires independent human auditor review.

## Human decisions

An auditor must inspect original sources, confirm source licensing and completeness, obtain authoritative IFRS material, evaluate client-specific facts, challenge comparator selection, and approve any accounting or audit conclusion.
