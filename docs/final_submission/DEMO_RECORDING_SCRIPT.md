# Demo recording script

Target length: 6–8 minutes. Do not expose private text, environment values, or full filings.

1. Introduce the auditor problem and state that the output is research support, not a conclusion.
2. Show the workflow banner and three source levels. Authoritative IFRS guidance and professional interpretation are missing.
3. Show 76 records/76 unique IDs and distinct raw/canonical hashes without opening the corpus.
4. Run the default property-development-loan/ECL scenario with `OPENROUTER_API_KEY` unset for a stable demonstration. Point out the truthful deterministic-fallback label.
5. Show Evidence needs and the six-action local trace.
6. Show Candidate discovery, distinguishing current local output from recorded Gate 5.1 SEC metadata.
7. Show Framework verification: Allied_REIT pending; Logistic Properties verified through issuer evidence; Form 20-F alone was not proof.
8. Show Comparability: Allied_REIT strong, Logistic Properties partial, Shinhan/Inter rejected for business mismatch.
9. State the caveat: Shinhan was over-rated in the single live output; hardening followed offline; there was no second live run.
10. Keep source-body expanders collapsed. Use chunk IDs/pages and external identifiers/non-verbatim metadata.
11. Show Authority/gaps and the missing official/professional layers.
12. Show the research handoff and mandatory human review.
13. Show frozen retrieval results separately from workflow evaluations and actual cost records.
14. End with privacy exclusions, limitations, and manual submission tasks.

Without private inputs, disable private mode and call the local content synthetic. The sanitized recorded result remains demonstrable without a network call.
